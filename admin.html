Storage Architecture
JSONBin stores ONLY: Trip ID, customer, addresses, rider, status, OTP (~1KB per trip). Never photos or signatures.
Excel file stores: All photos (base64), signature, invoice copy. Downloaded to rider's phone at trip close.
Result: JSONBin free tier (100KB/bin limit) will never fill up. Each bin stays under 2KB.
To clear JSONBin: Delete closed trip bins from jsonbin.io dashboard. Keep bt-config.
